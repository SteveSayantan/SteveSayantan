
# Summary: "English Speaking: Shopping For Clothes"

This video is a practical English lesson focused on vocabulary, phrases, and dialogues related to shopping for clothes. The lesson is structured in three main parts: deciding where to shop, shopping for clothes, and returning items. Each section introduces key vocabulary and then demonstrates their use in realistic conversations.



## **1. Introduction to Shopping Vocabulary**

-   **Difference between "go shopping" and "do the shopping":**
    
    -   _Go shopping_: Buying non-essential items (e.g., clothes, headphones).
        
    -   _Do the shopping_: Buying daily necessities (e.g., groceries, bread, dairy).
        
-   **Verb "shop" + preposition "for":**
    
    -   Use "shop for" when specifying what you’re buying (e.g., shop for clothes, shop for food).
        
        
## **2. Types of Clothing Stores**

We generally shop for clothes at:

-   **High Street Stores/Names:**
    
    -   Large shops selling mass-produced, reasonably priced clothes for ordinary people.
        
-   **Local Boutiques:**
    
    -   Small, fashionable shops located in your neighborhood, within walking distance.
        
-   **Out-of-Town Retail Outlets:**
    
    -   Large stores on the outskirts of the city, offering a wide variety of options; usually require a car to access.
        
-   **Designer Boutiques:**
    
    -   Sell expensive, branded clothes made by famous designers.
        
    -   _Designer labels_: High-priced, luxury items e.g. clothes, bags etc.
        
    -   _Idiom_: "Cost an arm and a leg" = very expensive.
        

## **3. Shopping for Clothes: Key Phrases and Dialogues**

-   **Salesperson Approaches:**
    
    -   "May I help you?" – Standard greeting from store staff.
        
    -   Response if not needing help: "I'm just browsing."
        
-   **Asking About Options:**
    
    -   "Does this come in [color/size]?" – Use "come in" to ask about different colors or sizes.
        
-   **Trying On Clothes:**
    
    -   "Try on" = put on clothes to see if they fit/look good.
        
    -   "Fitting rooms" = place in the store to try on clothes.
        
-   **Describing Fit and Appearance:**
    
    -   "It fits me" = correct size.
        
    -   "Too tight" / "Too loose" = not the right size.
        
    -   "It suits me/you" = looks good on someone.
        
-   **Clothes on Sale:**
    
    -   "On sale" = being sold at a reduced price.
        
    -   "Marked down from" = previous price has been reduced (e.g., $80 marked down from $100).
        


## **4. Returning Items: Vocabulary and Process**

-   **Reasons for Returning:**
    
    -   Wrong size (doesn't fit), doesn't suit you, or item is damaged (e.g., torn).
        
    -   _"Torn"_ = damaged by being pulled apart (e.g., The sleeve is torn).
        
-   **Return Process:**
    
    -   "Take back" = return an item to the store.
        
    -   Salesperson may ask: "May I have a look?"
        
    -   Always bring the _receipt_ (proof of purchase) and _price tag_.
        
-   **Exchange and Refunds:**
    
    -   "Exchange (something) for (something else)" = swap the item for another.
        
    -   "Refund" = get your money back.
        
        -   Verb: "The shop will refund me."
            
        -   Noun: "I want a refund." / "The shop will issue a refund."
